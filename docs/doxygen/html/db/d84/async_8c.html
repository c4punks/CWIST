Detailed Description

Deferred-response (async handler) implementation.

See include/cwist/net/http/async.h for the model. The dispatch path (app_serve_parsed_request) detects res->deferred right after the handler returns, acknowledges the handoff, and leaves req/res/fd/conn untouched; from then on the cwist_async completion path owns them.

Lifetime race: a foreign thread may complete (and thus want to free req/res/the handle) before the dispatch thread has observed res->deferred. The handoff is lock-free and rests on two rules:

  • Single writer. Until the ack only the dispatch thread touches req/res (GC disown, keep-alive) and the dispatch-side fields; the producer that wins the claim writes only its own result fields (r_*, final_res). respond()/abort()/timeout record their outcome there instead of editing res, and the deliverer applies it to res once both sides are done.
  • One state word. Dispatch sets ACKED and the winner sets PUBLISHED with acq_rel fetch_or on st; whichever arrives second sees the other's bit, has acquired the other's writes, and delivers. Exactly one side does.

◆ cwist_async_defer()

cwist_async * cwist_async_defer ( cwist_http_request *  req,
cwist_http_response *  res 
)

Defer the response for the current request.

Call from inside a route handler and return immediately afterwards; the handler must not touch req or res after this call. Ownership of both objects transfers to the returned handle. Framework middleware may finish its synchronous posthandler work before dispatch acknowledges the transfer. A foreign producer waits for that acknowledgement before mutating or sending the exchange. Do not join or otherwise wait for a completing producer from the handler/middleware. Inline completion claims and records the response, but sending and cleanup wait until dispatch/middleware unwind. This also permits inline abort when scheduling a producer fails, without another allocation or scheduler.

Returns
Handle to complete later, or NULL on allocation failure (the framework falls back to answering whatever the handler wrote).

◆ cwist_async_wait_dispatched()

void cwist_async_wait_dispatched ( cwist_async *  a)

Wait until dispatch has acknowledged the handoff of a.

A producer on another thread must not read or mutate the deferred request or response until dispatch and the middleware chain have unwound: until then the dispatch thread still reads the response (including res->deferred) and may send and destroy it. Call this on the producer before touching req/res directly; respond, respond_with and abort already wait on their own. Returns immediately on the dispatch thread itself (an inline producer runs before the acknowledgement by construction) and for NULL. The wait is short: dispatch acknowledges as soon as the handler and middleware return.
